Privacy

The app processes your documents locally on your computer.

Download

Saved datasets (cases) are stored only on your PC in %LOCALAPPDATA%\ForensicAuthorship. During uninstall, you are asked whether to delete them.

Updates

Network traffic is limited to update checks and update files from huggingface.co and github.com. Texts and documents are never sent. The app uses no analytics and requires no account. Turn off automatic startup checks in the Updates window.

Privacy

This site is static GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies of its own. Your theme preference is kept in localStorage.

Contact us through GitHub Issues.
